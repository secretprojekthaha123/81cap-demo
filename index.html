FAQ

Questions.

Male or Female?

81 MALE fits cans with female valves (400, 500, 600 ml and similar) and gives a 40–60 cm line depending on can pressure. 81 FEMALE fits ULTRA WIDE, MAD MAXX, BOOSTER and similar cans and gives an 81 cm line.

Is the cap reusable?

Yes. 81 CAP is made of high-quality factory-injected plastic and can be reused up to around 30 times without losing the quality of the output.

Which cans does it work with?

The maker tested 400, 600 and 750 ml cans. Pick MALE or FEMALE according to the valve of your can, see step 1 above.

Where do you ship?

Worldwide. EU orders arrive in 7–14 business days, orders outside the EU in 14–30 business days.

Does a bigger pack cost less per cap?

No. Every pack is €1.95 per cap, tax included. Bigger packs only save on shipping per cap.
