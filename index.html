FAQ

Questions.

Male or Female?

The name is the cap's connector. MALE caps have a pin and fit cans with a female valve (standard 400, 500, 600 ml cans). FEMALE caps have a socket and fit cans with a male valve (ULTRA WIDE, MAD MAXX, BOOSTER and similar). See the photos in the Male / Female guide.

Which cap for fill-ins, which for height?

81 CAP for the widest solid line and the fastest fill-ins. FIRE CAP for reach: a 20 cm dot up to 4.51 m high. 81X360° when you need to aim the spray at an angle, 5–45 cm wide.

Is the cap reusable?

81 CAP is made of high-quality factory-injected plastic and can be reused up to around 30 times without losing the quality of the output. The shop gives no reuse figure for FIRE CAP or 81X360°.

Which cans does 81 CAP work with?

The maker tested 400, 600 and 750 ml cans. Pick MALE or FEMALE according to the valve of your can.

Where do you ship?

Worldwide. EU orders arrive in 7–14 business days, orders outside the EU in 14–30 business days.

Does a bigger pack cost less per cap?

No. The price per cap is the same in every pack: €1.95 (81 CAP), €1.00 (FIRE CAP), €3.00 (81X360°), tax included. Bigger packs only save on shipping per cap.
